Privacy Policy

How we protect and respect your data

Last Updated: November 2024

1. Introduction

ShopAI ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Browsing Information

We track your browsing history, products you view, and items you add to your cart. This information is stored locally in your browser to provide personalized recommendations.

2.2 Cart & Order Information

When you make a purchase, we collect your name, email, phone number, shipping address, and payment information. This data is necessary to process and fulfill your order.

2.3 Device Information

We automatically collect information about your device, including IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited to improve our service performance.

2.4 Cookies

We use cookies to remember your preferences, maintain your session, and analyze how you use our platform. Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

  • Personalization: To provide AI-powered product recommendations tailored to your preferences
  • Service Improvement: To understand how you use our platform and improve our features
  • Order Processing: To fulfill your purchases and send order confirmations
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide assistance
  • Analytics: To analyze trends and measure the effectiveness of our marketing
  • Security: To protect against fraud and maintain a secure shopping environment

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of sensitive data during transmission
  • Secure password protocols
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request what personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (Right to be Forgotten)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Opt-out: Decline certain uses of your data for marketing

6. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties. We only share data with trusted partners who help us provide our service, such as payment processors and shipping providers. All partners are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

7. Local Storage

Most of your browsing data and preferences are stored locally in your browser using browser storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (e.g., through a purchase or form submission).

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@shopai.com

Address: 123 Tech Street, San Francisco, CA 94102

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of our service after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
